Calculus in eli5-style simple summary words

Suppose you are sailing ⛵️ across the pacific ocean to a tropical island 🏝️ (of your own survey). During the journey you recorded 📝 the hourly speed, daily distance, and total distance data. At the destination if you want to know the distance traveled you can simply refer to the distance log 📖. But what if distance info was not recorded or lost, in that case you’d have to use speed-time data to calculate✖️➕ the distance - accumulating all the products of speed-times-time (a product✖️ is essentially the area of a rectangle▭). What this means is: ➡️ If you have distance data, you can simply subtract numbers - easy! 😎 ➡️ If you only have speed data, then you have to accumulate areas - hard! 💦 Calculus techniques give you a math shortcut to find the easy solution when you only have the hard data. With calculus, area is simply difference. And hard is actually easy.
